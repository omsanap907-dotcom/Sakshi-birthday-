The Flower That Was Beautiful All Along 🌷

You are like a flower that is still too shy to bloom, perhaps hiding so much beauty within that the world has yet to discover the fragrance of your mind.

I don't see you as a flower that everyone admires only after it blooms. I see the beauty in you even now, before the world gets a chance to notice it.

You are a flower growing through the confusion of life, sometimes lost, sometimes stuck, still finding your way through the seasons. Yet somehow, even before you bloom, you carry a fragrance so beautiful.

Perhaps the world hasn't seen you the way you wish to be seen, or the way you truly deserve to be seen.

But I see a beauty in you that doesn't need to bloom to be real.

And maybe, one day, the world will finally stop and notice the flower that was beautiful all along.

I saw beyond what you expressed, beyond the beauty you showed the world.

I saw you, still stuck between the petals of a flower not yet bloomed.

I wanted to feel the wind around you, to feel the seasons that touched you, to understand the fragrance you carried even before you bloomed.

Not to change the flower, not to force its petals open, but to feel its beauty the way I saw you once.

— by a bee
who smelled your fragrance
before the world saw your flower. 🐝
